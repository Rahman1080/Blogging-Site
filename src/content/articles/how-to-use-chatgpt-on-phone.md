The ChatGPT app is free, works on both iPhone and Android, and gives you the same chatbot experience as the website plus a few things the browser can't do — like voice conversations and the ability to share a chat to your computer with one tap.

Here's how to install it, set it up, use voice mode, and keep your conversations in sync across devices.

## What you'll need

- An iPhone or Android phone
- An Apple ID or Google account (for app-store sign-in)
- Optionally, a ChatGPT account (you can create one after installing)

## How to install the ChatGPT app

The steps are nearly identical on both platforms.

### On iPhone

1. Open the **App Store** on your iPhone.
2. Tap the search tab and search for "ChatGPT" — the official app is published by OpenAI, and the description and developer name make that clear.
3. Tap **Get**, then confirm with Face ID or your password.
4. Wait for it to install, then open the app.

### On Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your phone.
2. Search for "ChatGPT" and look for the official app (published by OpenAI).
3. Tap **Install**, and wait for the download to finish.
4. Open the app when it's ready.

A note on fakes: there are plenty of third-party apps that copy the ChatGPT name. Use the official publisher's listing, and read the "About this app" section if you're unsure.

## How to sign in and get started

1. Open the ChatGPT app.
2. If you have an account, tap **Log in** and enter your details (or use the "Continue with Apple/Google" option on iPhone).
3. New to ChatGPT? Tap **Sign up** — you can create an account with an email address, or use your Apple or Google account. The free tier is enough to start.
4. Once you're in, you'll land on the chat screen. Type your question in the box at the bottom and send it.

New to prompting? Our [beginner guide to writing ChatGPT prompts](/articles/how-to-write-better-chatgpt-prompts) will get you better answers from the first message.

## How to use ChatGPT voice mode on your phone

Voice mode is where the phone app shines — you can talk to ChatGPT the way you'd talk to a person, and it replies out loud.

1. Tap the **headphones icon** (or the voice button) in the chat input area. On some versions it's labeled with a waveform or microphone icon.
2. Grant the microphone permission when the phone asks.
3. Start talking. The app transcribes your words, and you'll hear spoken responses.
4. To stop, tap the button again. The exact gesture varies by version.

Voice mode is handy for hands-free moments: cooking, walking, or just when typing feels slow. If voice isn't working, make sure the app has microphone permission in your phone's settings.

## How to use ChatGPT on your phone vs the website

The app and the website are the same underlying assistant, with a few differences:

- The app adds **voice mode** and push notifications.
- The app lets you **share a conversation to your computer** — useful when you start a question on the bus and want to continue on a laptop.
- The website is easier for long typing, copying code, and using browser extensions.
- Signing into the same account on both keeps your **conversation history in sync**, so you can continue any chat on either device.

## How to continue a conversation on your desktop

1. In the phone app, open the conversation you want to keep working on.
2. Tap the **share icon** (the square with an up arrow on iPhone; the overflow menu ⋮ on Android).
3. Choose **Copy link** (or "Share chat").
4. On your computer, sign into the ChatGPT website and paste the link into the browser — it opens that conversation, and any new messages appear in your history on both devices.

Alternatively, just log into the same account on your computer and the conversation is already there.

## Tips for getting the most from the app

- **Use the app's search.** Swipe down or use the search icon to find an old conversation by keyword instead of scrolling.
- **Combine voice and text.** Dictate a long question with your voice, then let it read the reply.
- **Start conversations you can finish later.** A question you start in line at the store becomes a draft you refine on your desktop.
- **Know where your data goes.** What you type in the app is stored like any chatbot conversation — check the privacy settings and keep sensitive information out. Our [guide to AI chatbot data privacy](/articles/ai-chatbot-privacy-data) covers what to avoid pasting.

## Troubleshooting quick hits

- **App won't log in?** Update the app, check your internet connection, and try again — or log in through the website to rule out an app-specific issue.
- **Voice mode silent?** Check microphone permissions in your phone's Settings, and make sure the phone's volume isn't muted.
- **App stuck or glitchy?** Force-close and reopen it. If problems persist, you can clear the app's cache without losing your conversation history — here's [how to clear app cache on Android and iPhone](/articles/how-to-clear-app-cache-android-iphone).
